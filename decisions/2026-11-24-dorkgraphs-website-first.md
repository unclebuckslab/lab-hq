# DorkGraphs is website first

Made Nov 15–24, 2026, in the DorkGraphs Project. This settles what the Nov 15 decision ([DorkGraphs gets its own section on the site](2026-11-15-dorkgraphs-on-the-site.md)) left open. The full record is the dorkgraphs repo's `ops/decision-log.md`.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| DorkGraphs is a publication of reports on the lab site, not an Instagram-only account. Instagram posts, carousels, Stories and Reels are cut from published reports and never add a number a report doesn't have | The work needed real stories with causes, not standalone charts; the site keeps them and social sends people to them |
| Cadence: one full report a week, plus short release-day pieces when a data release earns one. A week with nothing that clears the bar publishes nothing | Quality sets the pace |
| Four formats: full report, release-day piece, paper of the week, forecast vs. actual. The format is the kicker above the headline | Replaces the five Instagram series, which were built for a feed |
| Every lead needs a question someone has lived, a cause, and something new, and the founder approves each one. A Critic scores the finished report against a rubric built from his own scores | The first finding was correct and forgettable; the founder rated it 1.5/10 |
| The look is light only, teal (#0E7480), Inter, in the style of The Economist and Prof G. Charts keep that look on the site | The founder's pick; trust comes from the work, not decoration |
| Reports are written in the dorkgraphs repo and reach lab-site as pull requests opened by its publish script, so each gets a preview before it goes live. No token is stored at launch | Keeps every site change a reviewed PR; the private repo never has to be readable by the site build |
| The section is `/dorkgraphs` (a list of reports, a page per report, an RSS feed). Web charts come from the same spec as the Instagram post, with a phone version and a data table | One spec, two outputs, identical numbers |
| DorkGraphs and the lab site launch together on Monday, January 11, 2027 | One launch on a strong data calendar |
