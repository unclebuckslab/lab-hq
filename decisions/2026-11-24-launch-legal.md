# Launch legal choices: contact, email, list, paywall timing, holdings

Made Nov 24, 2026, in the planning Project, working through section (d) of the Nov 21 legal pages review. Not legal advice; the items marked lawyer still go to one.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **Postal address:** a virtual mailbox, set up by Garrett before signups open, and used as the LLC's business address in January | CAN-SPAM needs a valid postal address in every commercial email, and the welcome email sends on signup. A home address would be printed in every email |
| **Privacy contact:** `privacy@unclebuckslab.com`, forwarded through Cloudflare until lab email exists | Free; the Privacy page needs a contact |
| **Email tracking:** open tracking off, click tracking on, disclosed on the Privacy page | Clicks are the useful, accurate number; open counts are unreliable and need a hidden pixel |
| **beehiiv ad network, Boosts and Recommendations:** all off at launch | Keeps "we don't sell your data" true and betting or crypto ads out. Kept as possible growth tools later; Recommendations is the first to revisit |
| **One list:** the lab dispatch. The AI Money Report reaches readers as highlights in the dispatch and free previews on the site; its form says so, and the Privacy page matches | Matches the Nov 21 site-build decision; beehiiv free plan |
| **Paywall for the full AI Money Report:** planned, not at launch. It starts after a few free issues, once the LLC and a checkout that handles Washington sales tax are in place | No entity until January; the report needs a track record first. Whether a paid report stays inside the publisher exclusion goes to a lawyer |
| **Holdings:** disclosed on the site's Disclosures page, with a footnote link in each issue. Covered means named in that issue. Index funds exempt; accounts the founder holds, manages or directs count; accounts a partner manages herself don't; no dollar threshold. 7-day blackout either side of an issue stays | Simple to follow and defend. MONEY_RULES rule 3 is updated to match |
| **MONEY_RULES rule 5** reworded: reader data is never sold, and only the services that run the site and email handle it | "Never shared" was too absolute; beehiiv and Cloudflare both receive it |
