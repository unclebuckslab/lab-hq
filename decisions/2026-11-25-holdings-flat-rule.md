Made Nov 25, 2026, in the planning Project, from the outside review and the chief-of-staff walkthrough the same day. Approved by Garrett on Nov 25.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| MONEY_RULES rule 3 becomes: no individual trades in the AI Money Report's company universe. Index funds are exempt. Holdings are disclosed | A 7-day before/after window on a biweekly report is a permanent, unpredictable blackout that can't be followed reliably |

**Terms:**

- Covered companies: every company in the AI Money Report's 50-company universe, not only those named in an issue.
- Covered accounts: all of Garrett's own accounts.
- No threshold.
- Index funds exempt.
- Holdings disclosed.
