# DorkGraphs gets its own section on the site

Made Nov 15, 2026, in the planning Project.

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| DorkGraphs has its own brand section on the lab site, with weekly posts living under it | The site keeps the archive; DorkGraphs keeps its own identity inside the lab |
| DorkGraphs runs its own social, starting with Instagram (@dorkgraphs) | Social is where the brand grows; the site is where the work is kept |
| Posts come from the founder's existing DorkGraphs system, connected to the site later | The system already exists; the site plugs into it rather than replacing it |
| The section appears when DorkGraphs goes live, per the finished-work rule | Nothing in progress on the site |
| Still open: what a weekly post includes, which day it publishes, and the brand's look | Decided when the system is connected |
